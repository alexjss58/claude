# Market claims

One line per claim; source and confidence on each. Format in `research/README.md`.
Unless stated, the source is S4 (Horizon21 / SoftConstruct deck, 2026-02-09), which itself cites
the banks' press releases and "industry sources". Confidence is capped at **medium** for
anything transcribed from S4 without re-verification; figures are as of the deck date.

## Armenia

- **ArCa offers 2 % cashback on everything.** — source: S4 slide 16 · confidence: medium · implication: the flat-rate floor any category offer is judged against.
- **Armenian banks run time-boxed promos rather than structural cashback**: 100 % on public transport for the first 10 rides (9 Jan–31 Mar 2026); 5 % online for new users capped at 20k AMD/month; 2 % on Mastercard for new users (20 Jan–20 Mar 2026); partner cashback (Booking 7 %, KinoPark 15 %); idcoins loyalty points. — source: S4 slide 16, citing amiobank.am/ru/news/281 · confidence: medium · implication: the market signals cashback sensitivity but nobody has made it a permanent tier feature.
- **No Armenian player offers user-selected monthly cashback categories** (as of Feb 2026). — source: S4 slide 16 · confidence: medium, **re-verify before relying on it** — seven months old and the easiest claim for a competitor to invalidate · implication: the deck's proposed differentiator.
- **Competitor tariff comparison** (S4 slide 18; Card 2 = IDBank, Card 3 = Ameria per the slide notes, Card 1 unnamed): see `competitor-tariffs-2026-02.md`. — confidence: medium.

### Re-verified by the 2026-09-18 full scan

Full sweep of all six banks' live product pages and published tariffs; 104 cards captured,
100 in the debit comparison. Method, per-card records and gaps: `2026-09-18-competitor-cards-scan.md`.
These supersede the S4-transcribed Armenia claims above where they conflict.

- **No Armenian bank lets a cardholder choose their own cashback categories** — 0 of 100 cards. Falsification attempted in Armenian, Russian and English across the six scanned banks plus Acba, Inecobank, Unibank, AMIO, Araratbank, Armeconombank, ArmSwissBank, Idram and Telcell. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 (V5) · confidence: medium-high · implication: the deck's proposed differentiator survives re-verification, with an as-of date; it is also the claim a single competitor release could erase.
- **The "2 % cashback" in the Armenian market is the national ArCa scheme, not a bank offer** — 1 % Armenian Card + 1 % RA state budget from 1 Jan 2026, min transaction 1,000 AMD, cap 6,000 AMD per transaction and 60,000 AMD per month, attached to any ArCa-branded card at any issuer and structurally unavailable to Visa/Mastercard products. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 (V1) · confidence: high · implication: supersedes the S4 claim that "ArCa offers 2 % cashback on everything"; it is a floor nobody pays for and nobody can differentiate on.
- **Only 2 of 6 banks fund any cashback themselves** — 8 cards in total: IDBank (0.5 % flat on Signature/Infinite/Infinite SE; Travel Card 1.5 % travel / 0.5 % other foreign) and Evocabank (0.25 % local / 0.5 % abroad on Vision and Digital, 5,000 AMD cap; Travel Card 1.5 % / 0.5 %, 20,000 AMD cap). — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: high · implication: a bank-funded cashback on a Visa/MC product is close to a market first; the economics have no domestic precedent to lean on.
- **Armenian banks compete on the balance, not the spend** — 48 of 100 cards pay interest on an AMD balance. Best published rate per bank: FastBank 4.0 %, Evocabank 4.0 %, Conversebank 4.0 %, Ameriabank 3.0 %, Ardshinbank 3.0 %, IDBank none. Ardshinbank pays it on 27 of 28 cards (2.0 % classic, 3.0 % from Gold, 5m AMD threshold). — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: high · implication: FastBank's 4 % is already joint-best in the market and unthresholded; replacing it with cashback (S4 slides 25-27) gives up the bank's only leading position and must be priced as a trade, not assumed.
- **FastBank's published rate on card balances is 4 % nominal / 4.07 % APY, not 5 %** — bank-account tariff updated 09.07.2026; the 5 % figure survives only in a stale article title whose body says 4 %, and the 10.25 % is a 367-550 day term deposit. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 (V4) · confidence: high · implication: correct the deck's "5 % on balances" wherever it is carried forward.
- **A fee waiver is the domestic standard** — 68 of 100 cards publish one, 17 cards carry no fee at all. Average daily balance is the dominant mechanic (Ardshinbank 60k ArCa / 100k classic / 200k Gold-World / 500k Signature-Black; Conversebank 2m for any C360 package; IDBank packages at 50k balance or 100k spend), sometimes paired with monthly spend. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: high · implication: S2's "-" on every tier now diverges from the domestic market as well as the international benchmarks.
- **FastBank's premium tiers are priced far below the field** — Visa Infinite 50,000 AMD/yr against Ardshinbank 120,000 and IDBank 99,900; Gold 4,000 against 24,000 / 12,000; World 12,000 against 36,000 / 20,000. FastBank's tariffs also barely differentiate by tier: ATM, card-to-card and cash-in fees are identical from Visa Classic to Infinite Metal. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: high · implication: headroom on premium pricing, or a positioning choice that should be stated.
- **Preferential FX is published by 5 of 100 cards** — Evocabank's Travel Card is the clearest (0.5 dram margin on USD/EUR to ~5m AMD equivalent). — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: medium · implication: no competitor to beat, but no domestic evidence of demand either; belongs in customer discovery, not the tier spec.
- **Lounge, insurance and concierge are scheme-funded and spend-gated** — 21 of 100 cards carry lounge access, all via Visa Airport Companion/DragonPass or LoungeKey, typically gated on ~$400 of non-cash spend. Bank-funded travel insurance and Priority Pass were withdrawn at Ameriabank in 2023. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: medium-high · implication: cheap to match, differentiates nothing; do not spend design budget here.
- **Loyalty points are near-universal and near-worthless as money** — IDBank's idcoin is the strongest (1 idcoin = 1 AMD via Idram QR) but the whole balance is cancelled after 365 days without an idcoin payment; Converse Points "have no monetary expression and can never be exchanged for money", redeemable only for physical gifts on a 3-day branch coupon. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: medium · implication: points are a cost-deferral device in this market, not a perceived benefit; cash cashback reads as strictly better.
- **Exactly one airline co-brand exists in Armenia and it is a credit card** — IDBank Visa Wizz Air, launched 15 Sep 2026: 3 % on Wizz Air, 1.5 % on selected categories, 1 % elsewhere, cap 20,000 AMD/month, paid into the holder's Wizz Account wallet as airline scrip, plus EUR 20 welcome credit and free Wizz Discount Club membership. No other Armenian bank runs a carrier partnership. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 (V6) · confidence: medium · implication: the partner-collab space is effectively empty on the debit side.
- **IDBank Visa Signature costs 69,900 AMD/yr** — Armenian page and the official Debit Cards Information Bulletin agree; the English tariff tab's 69,000 contradicts its own page. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 (V2) · confidence: high.
- **Ameriabank's Platinum and Signature annual fees are disputed** — two reads of the same tariff PDF (edition 46, effective 07.07.2026), the only source since the product pages are JS-rendered, give 30,000 / 60,000 AMD on one reading and 60,000 / 40,000 on the other. — source: full scan 2026-09-18 · confidence: low · implication: both readings are on the card records; confirm with the bank before either enters a competitive pricing comparison.

## Patterns the benchmarks show (synthesis, not claims)

- Selectable categories with a monthly cap is the standard Russian-market mechanic (T-Bank, Alfa,
  VTB) — 3–5 categories, 5–15 %, cap ~30–60k RUB — and Plata's in Mexico. The bank's 3/5/7 split
  is more granular than any benchmark.
- **Every premium tier in the set waives its fee on a balance or spend condition.** S2 has no
  waiver at all. That is the sharpest divergence between the bank's draft and the market.
- Non-cashback perks that recur: interest on balance, lounges, concierge, travel insurance,
  bundled subscriptions, fee-free FX/ATM abroad, taxi rides. eSIM appears once (Nubank).
